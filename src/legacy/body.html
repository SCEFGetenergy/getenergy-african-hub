Conversion questions

Can I still use petrol after converting?

Yes. Petrol vehicles are converted to bi-fuel, so you can switch between CNG and petrol at any time.

Can diesel vehicles be converted?

Many diesel trucks and buses can run on a dual-fuel system that mixes CNG with diesel. We assess each vehicle before recommending it.

How long does a conversion take?

It depends on the vehicle and kit. We confirm the time at inspection, and fleet conversions are scheduled in batches.

Is CNG safe?

Yes, when installed and maintained properly. CNG is lighter than air and disperses quickly. Every conversion is leak and pressure tested, and cylinders need periodic inspection.

How much does conversion cost?

The price depends on the vehicle, the kit and the cylinder size. You receive a clear quotation after inspection, before any work begins.

Who we are

Built on operating experience

GetEnergy Trading Services Ltd (GETS) is a subsidiary of Pancokrato Integrated Services Ltd (PKIS). From 2023 through August 2026, diesel supply and commercial energy delivery formed a major part of our business, alongside community electricity vending delivered through implementation partners.

That operating experience — across construction, retail, healthcare, residential, hospitality and oil-exploration customers — built practical knowledge in procurement, logistics, customer accounts, metering, payments and reconciliation that now underpins our next phase.

Our direction

Engineering the transition to green energy

Since August 2026, GetEnergy has been redesigning how it operates — restructuring procurement, supplier management, customer onboarding, pricing, logistics, payments, CRM and metering into a scalable, interconnected energy-services ecosystem.

Green energy is a cross-cutting direction for that redesign: a practical pathway from reliable energy today, through greater efficiency and cleaner fuels, to smarter infrastructure and increasingly renewable, low-carbon energy across mobility, power, metering, storage and digital commerce.

Technology & metering learning

An honest account of what didn't work

GetEnergy explored electricity-meter manufacturing through a partnership arrangement that did not progress into the intended manufacturing operation. Rather than presenting this as an achievement, we treat it as an important business-learning experience — in technology partnerships, manufacturing requirements, supplier dependency, capital requirements, technical standards and commercial agreements — that now informs our more structured approach to smart-metering and OEM partnerships.

Business process engineering

Redesigning how we operate

Beginning in August 2026, GetEnergy commenced a broader programme of business-process engineering across procurement, supplier management, customer onboarding, pricing, quotations, logistics, payments, customer accounts, CRM, metering, reporting, compliance, data management, project development, partnerships and technology integration — turning individual energy activities into a scalable, interconnected ecosystem.

Frequently asked questions

What is GetEnergy?

GetEnergy Trading Services Ltd is a Nigerian energy company and a subsidiary of Pancokrato Integrated Services Ltd (PKIS). We offer electricity token vending and bill payments, diesel supply, CNG and EV services, Power as a Service, training and certification, and Energy E-Commerce Africa.

How do I buy electricity tokens?

Go to Get Electricity, choose your DISCO, and enter your meter number, amount, phone and email. Your token is sent by SMS and email once payment is confirmed. Online payment is launching soon; until then, send your request and our team will contact you to complete it.

Which DISCOs do you support?

AEDC, BEDC, EKEDC, EEDC, IBEDC, Ikeja Electric, JED, KAEDCO, KEDCO, PHED, YEDC and Aba Power.

What is Power as a Service?

We install and run a power system at your site, such as a generator, solar-hybrid, battery storage or CNG power, and you pay for the power you use instead of buying the equipment.

How do I order diesel?

Use the quote form on the Diesel page. We reply with a quote at the current depot price. Retail and commercial accounts have a monthly minimum of 20,000 litres.

Do you convert vehicles to CNG?

Conversion is a service we are building. Proposed centres in Lagos (Onipanu), Ibadan (Bodija) and Ilorin (Stadium Road) are not yet open, so you can register interest on the CNG Conversion page and we will contact you as each centre progresses.

What is Energy E-Commerce Africa?

EEA is our marketplace for Africa's energy industry, connecting buyers with verified suppliers, professionals, training providers and employers. The pilot launches in Lagos.

How secure is my information?

We only use your details to handle your request, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act. Payments are processed by a licensed payment provider, and we never store card details.
