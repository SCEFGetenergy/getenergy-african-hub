# Refactor the Academy positioning and delivery model

## Goal
Strengthen the “Green Skills • Technical Certification • Career Development” experience using the attached Nigerian training-centres reference, without removing the existing 74 products, certifications, waiting lists, student portal, payments, CPD, SOPHIA or admin tools.

## Changes
- Refine the Academy introduction so the three promises are clearer: green skills, competency-based certification and career development.
- Add a scannable six-area Academy structure based on the reference: Solar & Renewables; Power & Electrical; Energy Efficiency; Oil, Gas & CNG; EV & Battery; Advanced Energy.
- Explain the delivery model accurately: GET Energy operates the Academy platform, while suitable institutions may provide facilities, instructors, practical assessments or recognised certification only after programme-specific agreement and verification.
- Replace the broad partner-name list with a partnership-planning section grouped by energy area. Names from the PDF will be labelled as prospective organisations under evaluation, never as confirmed partners.
- Preserve the current catalogue, certification cards, career pathways, corporate training, portal and enquiry actions.
- Keep phone layouts compact and readable, with clean horizontal handling for any detailed partner information.

## Verification
- Check the Academy page at desktop and phone widths.
- Confirm the catalogue, waiting-list, corporate-training, portal and certification links still work.
- Confirm no prospective institution is presented as an approved partner or accrediting body.
- Check the latest build and browser errors before completion.

## Technical details
- Keep the main content in the existing Academy route and extract the new planning data into the Academy content module for reuse.
- Use the existing design tokens, shared sections and button components; no redesign of the wider site.
